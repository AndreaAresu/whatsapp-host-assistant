    How it works

    A single agentic call to Claude Haiku 4.5 with two tools: native web_search (geolocated on Costa Rei) and submit_response, through which the model hands back a structured decision instead of free text.

    Web search is switched off on this deploy, and that is a measurement, not an oversight. Netlify's free tier caps a function at 10 seconds of total execution, which streaming does not extend. Measured here: 2.4 to 3.5 seconds without search, 7.1 to 10.3 with it. Two questions out of three would die on the timeout, so DEMO_WEB_SEARCH=off drops the tool entirely rather than failing at random. The same pipeline runs it on the real bot, where no such limit applies.

    The house guide sits in its own system-prompt block with cache_control: from the second question on the input costs a tenth. The minimum cacheable prefix on Haiku 4.5 is 4096 tokens, and below it caching is skipped silently, so the guide is deliberately kept above that line.

    If the model never calls submit_response, the message is escalated. An uncertain case costs the host one message; a wrong one costs a guest's trust.

    The same pipeline runs on WhatsApp (Baileys) with approval over Telegram, plus photo understanding and voice-note transcription.

    Host inbox

    drafts awaiting approval · in the real bot these arrive on Telegram

    This is where replies land when the bot is not confident enough to send them itself: prices, dates, breakages, area information it hasn't verified.

    With REVIEW_EVERYTHING=true everything lands here, even the safe replies: it's the safety valve for the first days in production.
